Early Life and Education

Jung Kook was born on 1 September 1997 in Busan, South Korea. He developed an interest in music and performance from a young age.

Career

Jung Kook debuted as a member of BTS in 2013. He has participated in many BTS albums, songs, concerts and music videos.

As a solo artist, he released several successful songs and his first solo album, GOLDEN, in 2023.
